**A pipeline thread retries a full shard queue sooner** (`spate-core`)

A push or drain flush that a full shard queue refused now waits 200 µs before its first retry, and the wait doubles on each further refusal up to `RuntimeOptions::blocked_retry`, 2 ms by default. In previous versions every retry waited the full 2 ms, so with many pipeline threads on few shards the source could fall behind while the queue had room. Each retry that still finds the queue full counts in `spate_queue_full_events_total`, so the counter rises faster under the same contention, and an alert on its rate may need a higher threshold. A not-ready payload still replays after `blocked_retry`.
