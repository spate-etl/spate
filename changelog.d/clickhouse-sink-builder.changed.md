**Breaking:** **The ClickHouse sink builds through `ClickHouseSinkBuilder::new` and `ClickHouseSinkBuilder::from_component_config`** (`spate-clickhouse`)

`ClickHouseSinkBuilder::from_component_config(section)` and `ClickHouseSinkBuilder::new(config)` replace the free functions `spate_clickhouse::from_component_config` (also at `spate_clickhouse::config::from_component_config`) and `spate_clickhouse::config::build`, which are removed. `ClickHouseSinkBuilder` is exported at the crate root. The builder's `with_row::<F>()` is renamed `connect::<F>()`, because it reads the table's schema from every replica. The schema read and the settings check are unchanged, and so are its errors, except that under `format: native` it also refuses a column whose type the Native format does not write.

`ClickHouseSinkConfig` gains two public methods. `validate()` checks the configuration rules without creating a client or reading `tls.root_ca`, and its errors start at `sink.clickhouse`. `from_component_config(section)` deserializes and validates a section, and its errors start at the section's path, such as `sinks.eu.clickhouse.shards: must not be empty`.
