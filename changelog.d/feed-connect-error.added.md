**`spate_core::feed::ConnectError` for a feed backend that cannot connect** (`spate-core`)

A feed backend's `connect` can return `ConnectError`. `Config` carries a `feed:` section the backend cannot read. `Refused` means starting again fails the same way, and `Unavailable` means a later start may succeed. Each names the backend in its text, such as `the Kafka feed cannot start: …` or `Kafka is unavailable: …`. A backend builds one with `ConnectError::refused`, `ConnectError::unavailable`, or `ConnectError::from_class`, which maps `ErrorClass::Retryable` to `Unavailable` and `Fatal` or `RecordLevel` to `Refused`.
