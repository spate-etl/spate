**A stopping worker reads its feed units to a reserve before the drain deadline** (`spate-core`, `spate-test`)

On a graceful stop, a source whose feed hands back receives no more units and keeps reading the ones it holds until each is read to its end or until 5 s before `checkpoint.drain_timeout` runs out. It then hands back the units it has not read to its end, so another instance can take them at once. The drain deadline counts from the stop. The drivers and the sink drain end one second before it, and after the final commit the runtime closes the feed within what is left. Startup refuses a `checkpoint.drain_timeout` of 5 s or less with such a feed. Sources and feeds that do not opt in stop as before. A feed backend opts in through `Feed::hands_back`, `Feed::stop_receiving`, `Feed::hand_back` and `Feed::close`, and a source through `Source::reading_on_stop` and `Source::stop_reading`; `spate_core::feed::STOP_RESERVE` is the 5 s reserve, and `FeedScript::hand_back_on_stop` lets a test play such a feed.
