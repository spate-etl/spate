**A bounded feed job checks its job fingerprint at a job location** (`spate-s3`)

When the `feed:` section names a `location`, the S3 source treats the job as bounded. Before it receives a unit, each worker reads the job fingerprint at that object-store prefix and compares it with its own. The fingerprint covers the unit format version, the source `url`, `compression`, `split_target_bytes` and the framer's delimiter. A worker whose fingerprint differs fails startup with both in the error, and one that finds none within five minutes exits non-zero. A location is written as `s3://<bucket>/<prefix>`, `s3a://<bucket>/<prefix>` or `file://`, and the source refuses any other form, one that carries userinfo, a query or a fragment, and one at or under its own prefix.
