**A bounded feed job checks its job fingerprint at a job location** (`spate-s3`)

When the `feed:` section names a `location`, the S3 source runs a bounded job whose lister keeps its state at that object-store prefix. Before it receives a unit, each worker reads the job fingerprint the lister wrote there and compares it with its own. The fingerprint covers the unit format version, the source `url`, `compression`, `split_target_bytes` and the framer's delimiter. A worker whose fingerprint differs fails startup with both in the error, and one that finds none within five minutes exits non-zero. The source refuses a location at or under its own prefix, and one on a scheme it cannot open.
