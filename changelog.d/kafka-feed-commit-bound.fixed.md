**A Kafka feed's commit waits a bounded time for its group's answer** (`spate-kafka`)

A Kafka feed's commit waits up to a quarter of `max.poll.interval.ms`, at most 10 seconds, for the group's answer, and the last commit of a close waits no longer than the close's deadline. A commit with no answer in that time counts as failed, and the feed sends no other commit until the group answers it. A close whose last commit goes unanswered settles as failed, so the units that commit would have finished are read again, and the consumer leaves the group in the background. In previous versions a commit waited for as long as the coordinator stayed silent or answered slower than `socket.timeout.ms`, so the feed's consumer thread stopped polling, a close never settled, and dropping the feed never returned.
