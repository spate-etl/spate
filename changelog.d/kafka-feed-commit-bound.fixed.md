**A Kafka feed's commit waits a bounded time for its group's answer** (`spate-kafka`)

A Kafka feed's commit waits up to a quarter of `max.poll.interval.ms`, at most 10 seconds, for the group's answer, and the last commit of a close waits no longer than the close's deadline. A commit with no answer in that time counts as failed, and the feed sends no other commit until the group answers it. A close whose last commit goes unanswered settles as failed, so the units that commit would have finished are read again, and the consumer leaves the group in the background. A periodic commit's wait ends when a close arrives, a revocation during a close waits no later than the close's deadline, and a close of a feed that stopped on a fatal error waits for the consumer's close no later than that deadline either. In previous versions a commit waited for as long as the coordinator stayed silent or answered slower than `socket.timeout.ms`, so the feed's consumer thread stopped polling, a close never settled, and dropping the feed never returned.

A close's wait for its writes in flight now ends once half of what the close was given has passed since it began. It used to take half from when the feed's consumer thread took the close, so a close that arrived while the thread read an assignment's committed offsets waited longer.
