**Dropping a Kafka feed returns after a fatal consumer error** (`spate-kafka`)

Dropping a `KafkaFeed` that stopped on a fatal consumer error now returns within 10 seconds, and its consumer's close goes on in the background after that. In previous versions the drop waited for that close, which after some fatal errors never returns, such as a broker answering the feed's member with `FENCED_INSTANCE_ID`. librdkafka's main thread then held one core and a pipeline holding the feed's only handle never returned from `run`. No record was lost: the feed commits nothing after a fatal error, and the next owner reads on from the last commit.
