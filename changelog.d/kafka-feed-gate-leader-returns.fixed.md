**A Kafka feed reads an attempt partition again once its leader returns** (`spate-kafka`)

When a watermark read of an attempt partition fails, the Kafka feed's gate now asks the broker for the attempt topic's metadata before its next pass. A pass that also ran out of time waiting for the attempt follower leaves this to the next pass. Once the partition has a leader again, the gate reads it within a few passes and admits its units. In previous versions librdkafka kept the answer that the partition had no leader, and every later read failed with `UnknownPartition` until that cache entry expired after `metadata.max.age.ms`, 15 minutes by default. The partition's units waited at the gate meanwhile, and once 128 units waited the feed paused every work partition. A pass whose reads all succeed sends no extra request.
