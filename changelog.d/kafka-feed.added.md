**A Kafka feed backend** (`spate-kafka`, `spate`)

`spate_kafka::feed::KafkaFeed` reads work units from Kafka topics. `KafkaFeed::connect` builds it from a `feed: { kafka: … }` section, with `brokers`, `group_id`, the work `topic`, `retry_topic`, `dead_letter_topic`, `attempt_topic`, `delivery_timeout` (default 30 s), `statistics_interval` (default 5 s) and an `rdkafka:` passthrough, and code passes it to `Pipeline::feed`. Every worker reads the work and retry topics as one consumer group with eager assignment, and a publish writes a unit to the work topic with the unit's id as the key. Before a unit is read, the feed writes its attempt count to the compacted attempt topic as `{"v":1,"attempts":n}`, keyed by the publish identity `<topic>/<partition>/<offset>`, and the unit is read with that count once the write is confirmed. Every read counts, including one whose worker dies, though two copies of one publish read at the same time, as after a rebalance, can count once between them. A unit whose recorded attempts have reached the section's `attempt_cap` (default 10) goes to the dead-letter topic without being read. An acknowledged unit's attempt record is tombstoned, and its offset is committed once every record in it is acknowledged and the tombstone is confirmed. A failed unit is written to the retry topic, or to the dead-letter topic at the cap, with `spate.origin` and `spate.deliveries` headers, and its offset is committed only once that write is confirmed, and for the dead-letter topic once the tombstone after it is. A message with no key, or a key that is not UTF-8, goes to the dead-letter topic at once. The feed never creates a topic. `KafkaFeed::connect` reads the four topics' metadata and configuration first, within 30 seconds, and refuses a missing topic, a work, retry or dead-letter topic whose `cleanup.policy` includes `compact`, an attempt topic whose `cleanup.policy` is anything but `compact` or that reports no partitions, and a work topic whose `message.timestamp.type` is not `LogAppendTime`, so the work topic's retention counts from when the broker appends each message, also for a store that stamps its notifications with timestamp 0. For a topic that goes missing after the feed connects, a write librdkafka still holds when it marks the topic missing stops the pipeline. After that, a publish to the missing work topic is reported to its caller as retryable, and an attempt, tombstone, retry or dead-letter write is sent again until the topic exists, with its partition committing nothing past it meanwhile. Dropping the feed waits up to 1 s for writes in flight before its last commit. `KafkaFeedPublisher::connect` builds a publish-only handle from the same section, for a process that only publishes, such as a lister run: it runs the same startup checks, then writes units to the work topic through one producer with the feed's settings, joins no group, reads no unit and writes no attempt record. Its `receive` fails as Fatal, and dropping it reports each publish it has not confirmed as retryable. Its identity needs `Describe` and `DescribeConfigs` on the four topics and `Write` on the work topic. A credential a broker rejects, or a TLS handshake either side rejects, after the feed or the handle connected, as its producer reports it, stops it with the reason, and so does a fatal producer error such as a refused idempotent producer id. `rdkafka:` refuses the properties the feed owns, such as `group.id`, `acks` and `partition.assignment.strategy`.
