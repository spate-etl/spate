**A Kafka feed backend** (`spate-kafka`, `spate`)

`spate_kafka::feed::KafkaFeed` reads work units from Kafka topics. `KafkaFeed::connect` builds it from a `feed: { kafka: … }` section, with `brokers`, `group_id`, the work `topic`, `retry_topic`, `dead_letter_topic`, `delivery_timeout` (default 30 s), `statistics_interval` (default 5 s) and an `rdkafka:` passthrough, and code passes it to `Pipeline::feed`. Every worker reads the work and retry topics as one consumer group with eager assignment, and a publish writes a unit to the work topic with the unit's id as the key. A unit's offset is committed once every record in it is acknowledged. A failed unit is written to the retry topic with a `spate.deliveries` header, or to the dead-letter topic at the section's `attempt_cap` (default 10), and its offset is committed only once that write is confirmed. The count is of reported failures only: a unit whose worker dies is read again without being counted. A message with no key, or a key that is not UTF-8, goes to the dead-letter topic at once. The feed never creates a topic. `KafkaFeed::connect` reads the three topics' metadata and configuration first, within 30 seconds, and refuses a missing topic and one whose `cleanup.policy` includes `compact`. For a topic that goes missing after the feed connects, a write librdkafka still holds when it marks the topic missing stops the pipeline. After that, a publish to the missing work topic is reported to its caller as retryable, and a retry or dead-letter write is sent again until the topic exists, with its partition committing nothing past it meanwhile. `rdkafka:` refuses the properties the feed owns, such as `group.id`, `acks` and `partition.assignment.strategy`.
