**A Kafka source keeps its prefetch through a short pause** (`spate-kafka`)

When the runtime pauses a lane, the Kafka source now pauses the partition at the consumer only once the lane has stayed paused for 2 seconds. A pause lifted sooner keeps the messages the consumer had prefetched, and the partition is not fetched again. In previous versions every pause purged the prefetch, so under a sink that paused and resumed the source several times a second the consumer could receive each message about twice. Until a pause reaches the consumer, the partition keeps fetching up to `queued.min.messages` and `queued.max.messages.kbytes`, and on a topic with aborted transactions those limits do not count the aborted records. A partition held at `checkpoint.max_pending_batches` now stops fetching at least `backpressure.min_pause` plus 2 seconds after it reaches the limit.
