**`Pipeline::run` stops waiting for a source or feed to close 3 seconds past the drain deadline** (`spate-core`)

After the final commit, `run` waits for the source and the feed to close until 3 seconds past `checkpoint.drain_timeout`, or 3 seconds past a final commit that ends later. A close still running then is logged at `WARN`, and `run` returns its `ExitReport` while the close goes on in the background with the I/O runtime. In previous versions `run` waited for the close with no limit, so a source or feed whose `Drop` never returned kept `run` from returning. A process that exits once `run` returns stops such a close part way, and a consumer group it was leaving waits out its session timeout. `spate_core::task::ThreadHandle` gains `join_by`, which waits for the thread until a deadline on a given clock, and `is_finished`.
