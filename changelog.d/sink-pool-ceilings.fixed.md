**Breaking:** **`batch.linger` and `inflight.max_per_shard` have ceilings** (`spate-core`)

A sink whose `batch.linger` is longer than a year, or whose `inflight.max_per_shard` is above tokio's `Semaphore::MAX_PERMITS`, fails pipeline load with an error that names the key. In previous versions such a value loaded. A `max_per_shard` over the limit panicked the shard worker as it started, and a `linger` past about 292 billion years panicked it on the first chunk. `SinkPool::spawn` panics on an out-of-range `max_per_shard` as it does on zero, and seals a `linger` over a year at a year. `RateLimit` takes a window of any length. The new `clock::deadline_after` adds a duration to a clock reading with the duration capped at `clock::LONGEST_DEADLINE`, one year.
