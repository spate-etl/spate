**`spate-sqs`, an Amazon SQS feed backend** (`spate-sqs`, `spate`)

A new crate, `spate-sqs`, behind the `spate` feature `sqs`, reads work units from an SQS standard queue. `SqsFeed::connect` builds the feed from a `feed: { sqs: … }` section, with `queue_url`, `visibility` (default 60 s, renewed every third of it), `region` and `endpoint`, and code passes it to `Pipeline::feed`. A received unit stays hidden while its reader renews it, and the feed clamps every extension to the 12 hours SQS holds a message from the receive that issued its receipt handle. A unit is deleted once every record in it is acknowledged, and a failed unit is made visible at once, so the queue's redrive policy moves a unit that keeps failing to its dead-letter queue. A unit's body travels as the message text and its id in the `spate.unit` message attribute. `attempt_cap` is refused on `sqs`, where the queue's `maxReceiveCount` counts deliveries.
