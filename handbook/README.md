# Handbook

How Spate writes for readers outside the code: what each kind of writing is
for, who reads it, and the rules a piece follows before it is published. Each
chapter is the only place its rules are stated. The mechanics of the place a
piece is published, such as front matter and build checks, stay beside it and
link here.

- [Blog posts](blog-posts.md): choosing a topic, the title and description,
  the shape of each kind of post, evidence, writing about other tools, voice,
  and where a post is shared.
