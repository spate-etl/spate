The Spate mark is the word `spate` in IBM Plex Sans, cut once by a swell. The letters above the water are ink and the letters below it take the accent. The icon is the wordmark's first letter, framed from the same artwork. Every file on this page comes from the [brand generator in the repository](https://github.com/spate-etl/spate/tree/main/website/tools/brand), and none is edited by hand.

## Wordmark and icon

The wordmark is the logo. Use it wherever the name has to be read. Use the icon where the space is square or too small for the wordmark.

## The water

- Ink fills the letters above the water and the accent fills them below, on both grounds.
- The water is a channel of the ground cut through the letters. It is never drawn as a line or filled with a color.
- The water sits at one height. A height that changed with a page or a status would read as a measurement.
- The mark takes no gradient, no fade on the part below the water, no ripples or droplets, and no animation.

## Carriers

A carrier is a large numeral cut by the same water: Overpass Mono SemiBold, ink above and the accent below, with the water at 0.72 of the numeral's ink height on every surface. The numeral names something the surface counts, such as the four stages of a pipeline, a section's place in a sequence, or a status code. It never states a result, a throughput or a rank, so the benchmark's social card carries none.

- The social card carries `04`, for the four stages: Extract, Transform, Load and Observe.
- The 404 page carries `404`.
- In the slide deck, the title slide carries `04` and a section slide carries its section's ordinal.

On documentation pages, a carrier can mark the page header or a major section:

- A section carrier marks a major H2 transition on a long page. It never appears at an H3 or H4, between code blocks, inside a table or above every paragraph. A page whose header carries a carrier takes none at its first section.
- The numeral is an ordinal: the page's or the section's place in its sequence.
- In a content column narrower than 640 px, the carrier moves above the heading at 76 × 70 px, with 24 px between the carrier and the heading.
- The carrier is decorative and hidden from assistive technology with `aria-hidden="true"`. The heading beside it is live text, and no text enters the water.

## Type

IBM Plex Sans sets text and Overpass Mono sets code, figures and carriers, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Headings are SemiBold with tight tracking; body text is Regular at 16 px on a 1.6 line height. The wordmark is Plex Sans SemiBold at −0.022 em, baked to outlines, so it never depends on an installed font.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space on every side of the wordmark and the icon equals the depth of the water: the height of the part of the `s` below it. Measured from the generated artwork, that is 0.19 of the wordmark's ink height and 7.2 units on the icon's 32-unit canvas.
- The wordmark's ink is at least 96 px wide. Below that, use the icon.
- The icon is at least 16 px.

## Slides

The [Spate starter deck](/brand/spate-slides.pptx) is a PowerPoint file with title, section, content, code and closing slides on both grounds. Its text is set in IBM Plex Sans and Overpass Mono, Regular and Medium. Install both before editing it:

- IBM Plex Sans: the static TTF files in `ibm-plex-sans.zip` from [IBM Plex releases on GitHub](https://github.com/IBM/plex/releases).
- Overpass Mono: the static files from [Overpass Mono on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Overpass+Mono).

## Using the name and the mark

The name Spate and the mark identify this project. Use them to refer to it: in articles, talks, documentation, comparisons and criticism, and to say that your software works with Spate. Do not use them in a way that suggests the project sponsors, endorses or is affiliated with you or your product. Do not use the mark in your own product's name or logo, and do not alter it. A fork takes its own name and mark. The Apache-2.0 license covers the code and grants no rights to the name or the mark ([section 6 of the Apache-2.0 license](https://github.com/spate-etl/spate/blob/main/LICENSE)).

## Naming

Spate is written with a capital S in prose and in lowercase in the wordmark.
