This site counts page views with [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), to see which pages are read and which sites send readers here. It is the only analytics on the site.

## What a page view sends

A script on each page sends Cloudflare a short report on each page view, at `cloudflareinsights.com`. The report holds:

- the page's address, and the address of the page that linked to it;
- timings of how the page loaded and rendered, and how much memory its scripts used;
- your browser's engine and version, and your operating system's version.

Like any request, the report also carries your IP address.

## What it does not do

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare [states](https://blog.cloudflare.com/privacy-first-web-analytics/) that it does not track visitors over time by IP address, user agent or any other attribute for analytics. A visit is counted as a page view whose link came from another site.

## Opting out

Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser. The site then does not load the analytics script, and your page views are not counted.
